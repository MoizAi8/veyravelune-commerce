# The Royal Realm — direction and camera score

Reviewed the live sites in Edge at 1280 × 800, at entry and after two wheel movements. Oryzo and Lusion needed a second visit with a longer loading interval. Observations cover these desktop samples.

- Oryzo holds the round coaster in the viewer's attention as its surroundings change from a gridded work surface to an isolated dark field. In the Realm, the camera carries a silhouette across the change of light. [Oryzo](https://oryzo.ai/) · [Studio project](https://lusion.co/projects/oryzo_ai/)
- Lusion frames moving blue and pale solids in a restrained editorial page. Scrolling crops the image and introduces a large two-line statement beneath it. The Realm releases its final room into a full-width collection spread using native scroll. [Lusion](https://lusion.co/)
- Lando opens in intense yellow-green, then overlaps a signature, a portrait and large moving letterforms. A small constant menu anchors the changing scale. Our architectural entrance uses a single oversized house title against an amber oculus. [Lando Norris](https://landonorris.com/) · [OFF+BRAND](https://www.itsoffbrand.com/our-work/lando-norris)
- The supplied Codrops article returned HTTP 403. The three live sites supplied sufficient direction. No reference artwork or code was imported.
